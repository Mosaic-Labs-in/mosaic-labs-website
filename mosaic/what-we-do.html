Core Capabilities

The Architecture of Real-World Data.

Three integrated capabilities that turn fragmented physical reality into clean, structured, and auditable datasets for artificial intelligence.

++
01 Acquisition

Multi-Channel Sourcing

We acquire physical-world data across three primary channels: curated public repositories, targeted on-demand capture campaigns, and exclusive data partnerships with domain operators.

  • Curated open-access & public repository ingestion with copyright auditing
  • Custom field capture campaigns: aerial drones, LiDAR, dashcams, and IoT sensors
  • Commercial partnership networks with fleet, utility, and infrastructure operators
02 Quality & Verification

Annotation & Structuring

Raw sensor signals are normalized, cleaned, and labeled to exacting standards. Automated PII redaction and domain-expert validation ensure privacy and benchmark-grade accuracy.

  • Automated privacy redaction: facial blurring & license plate masking (DPDP-aligned)
  • Domain-expert annotation: 2D/3D bounding boxes, semantic segmentation & spatial metadata
  • Multi-tier QA: automated statistical validation + human expert peer review
03 Delivery & Intelligence

Data as a Service & Models

We deliver structured datasets formatted for instant AI model consumption, provide ongoing subscription feeds that refresh as reality changes, and train applied domain models directly.

  • Immediate format ingestion: COCO, YOLO, GeoJSON, Parquet, and custom schemas
  • Ongoing subscription feeds that continuously refresh as physical environments change
  • Specialized applied AI models tuned directly to your operating domain
