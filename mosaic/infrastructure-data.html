Enterprise Domain 02 // Infrastructure

Inspection Data Labeling & Quality Control

Infrastructure owners and survey contractors collect road surface scans, bridge and dam drone imagery, and highway dashcam footage, but defect review takes far longer than capture. Mosaic Labs reviews, annotates, and verifies existing inspection imagery against standard defect taxonomies and IRC standards, delivering structured datasets formatted directly to client specifications.

DATA INTAKE: ROAD SCANS · DRONE IMAGERY · DASHCAM FOOTAGE TAXONOMIES: IRC CODES · CLIENT DEFECT LISTS
OUTPUT: STRUCTURED DEFECT DATASETS
++
Domain Overview

The Data Bottleneck in Physical Infrastructure

Automated survey vehicles can capture hundreds of kilometres of highway a day, and drones routinely record thousands of high-resolution images of bridges and dams. However, turning raw visual records into actionable engineering data requires reviewing each frame for distress, categorizing defects by severity, and ensuring consistent reporting across entire networks.

01 / VOLUME

Repeating Survey Volumes

Regular network surveys on national highways and state roads generate vast image sequences that quickly outpace manual in-house review bandwidth.

02 / TAXONOMY

Defect Standardization

Defects must be classified by type and severity against standardized criteria (such as IRC codes) to be legally valid and engineering-ready.

03 / QUALITY

Verification & QA

Multi-reviewer sampling and consistency checks verify label accuracy, while correcting false detections from automated computer vision tools.

++
Capabilities & Deliverables

What We Deliver

A systematic data processing service transforming raw visual inspection feeds into verified defect records.

01 / DATA INTAKE

Inspection Imagery

Road surface scans, vehicle-mounted camera frames, drone photography of bridges and dams, and highway dashcam footage collected during field surveys.

02 / ANNOTATION

Defect Labeling

Systematic marking and classification of defects—including cracks, potholes, ravelling, spalling, and corrosion—graded by type and severity against standard defect lists.

03 / VERIFICATION

Quality Control

Rigorous sampling and reviewer checks measuring agreement with reference standards. Includes human verification and correction of automated AI detection outputs.

04 / OUTPUT

Structured Datasets

Cleaned, verified defect datasets mapped to client-specified defect codes, database schemas, and export formats ready for maintenance management systems.
