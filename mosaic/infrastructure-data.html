Enterprise Domain 04 // Infrastructure

Inspection Data Labeling & Quality Control

Infrastructure owners and survey contractors collect road surface scans, drone imagery, and highway footage, but manual defect review takes far longer than capture. Mosaic Labs provides processing labor and quality control on existing inspection data, delivering standard-compliant defect classifications in client-specified formats.

FOCUS: EXISTING INSPECTION DATA PROCESSING STANDARDS: IRC / CLIENT DEFECT LISTS
ROLE: SUBCONTRACTOR CAPACITY PARTNER
++
The Bottleneck

Collection scales easily. Labeling does not.

A survey vehicle can capture hundreds of kilometres a day on national highways and state roads, and drones regularly inspect bridges and dams under dam safety rules. However, reviewing each image takes far longer. Survey contractors are paid for the survey itself and rarely build or maintain an in-house labeling team.

General annotation firms usually lack road and bridge defect knowledge, while in-house staff are slow and expensive. Mosaic fills this capacity gap with dedicated review labor, domain expertise (such as IRC standards), and strict quality control.

Key Operational Realities
  • You collect nothing: We process data that survey firms and infrastructure owners already hold.
  • Contractual confidentiality: Work is executed under contractor terms with NDAs and secure data handling.
  • AI assistance positioning: Checking and correcting AI defect detection outputs alongside manual labeling.
  • Time-sensitive need: Regular, repeating surveys on highways and mandatory bridge/dam structures.
++
Delivery Pipeline

What We Do & What We Deliver

A direct services workflow: receiving existing raw imagery, annotating defects against client codes, and returning cleaned, verified data.

01 / INPUTS

Inspection Imagery

Road surface scans, survey vehicle camera images, drone images of bridges and dams, and highway dashcam footage collected by contractors.

02 / PROCESSING

Defect Labeling

Reviewing each image, marking defects (cracks, potholes, ravelling, spalling, corrosion), and classifying by type and severity against standard defect lists.

03 / VERIFICATION

Quality Control

Sampling and reviewer process measuring agreement with reference labels. Consistency and turnaround time are the core deliverables.

04 / OUTPUTS

Client-Format Data

Cleaned, structured defect datasets formatted to client defect codes and export tools. Quoted per image, kilometre, or structure.

SAMPLE PILOT OFFER

300 to 500 Images / Sample Kilometres

A starter pilot to demonstrate quality: send 300 to 500 inspection images or a few kilometres of road data. We label against your defect list and return completed files in your export format with an agreement quality report within a few days.
