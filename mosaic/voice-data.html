Enterprise Domain 05 // Speech Datasets

Voice Data Collection for AI: Hindi, English and Gujarati

Building reliable speech technology requires speech data that reflects real-world communication across regional accents, dialects, and everyday conversational styles. Mosaic Labs sources, prepares, and structures high-quality voice datasets across Hindi, Indian English, and Gujarati—including regional dialects and spontaneous code-switching.

LANGUAGES: HINDI · INDIAN ENGLISH · GUJARATI DIALECTS: KATHIYAWADI & NORTHERN VARIATIONS
FORMATS: AUDIO FILES · TIME-ALIGNED VERIFIED TRANSCRIPTS

Domain Overview

Why Language and Dialect Diversity Matter

A speech model trained on textbook translations or standard accents frequently degrades when encountering regional pronunciation, conversational pacing, and mixed language usage. High-quality speech AI requires datasets built from how people actually communicate in real settings, supported by clear audio, verified transcriptions, and documented linguistic criteria.

01 / DIALECTS

Authentic Dialect Coverage

Capturing regional linguistic variations—such as Kathiyawadi and Northern Gujarati—through reviewed speaker backgrounds rather than inferring dialect from geography alone.

02 / CODE-SWITCHING

Natural Language Mixing

Recording spontaneous transitions between Hindi-English and Gujarati-English to represent how multilingual speakers communicate in everyday conversation.

03 / GOVERNANCE

Consent & Data Governance

Ethical speaker recruitment with explicit informed consent, clear usage terms, secure data handling, and transparent collection documentation.

PROCESSING & STRUCTURING PIPELINE

From Recording to Verified Dataset

Recordings undergo systematic audio quality checks for background noise, clipping, and incomplete speech. Processing includes segmentation, transcription, language identification, and dialect labeling. Deliverables include cleaned audio files, verified transcripts, speaker metadata, and documentation formatted to client specifications.
