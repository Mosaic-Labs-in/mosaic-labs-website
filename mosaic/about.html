About Mosaic Labs

"We're building the layer between the physical world and intelligence."

The computational foundation that allows artificial intelligence models to perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world with absolute mathematical assurance.

++
OUR MISSION

Make real-world data computable.

For decades, compute advanced while physical sensor capture remained fragmented, analog, and prone to environmental degradation. We engineer hardware-precise time synchronization, deterministic denoising, and continuous spatial calibration so machines operate on uncompromised truth.

OUR VISION

A world where physical systems can think, adapt and improve.

When autonomous fleets, electrical grids, and urban infrastructure operate on synchronized ground-truth data, physical operations transition from reactive maintenance to self-healing autonomy.

++
Engineering Philosophy

The Law of Physical Ground Truth

Data from the digital realm is clean, discrete, and orderly. Data from the physical world is messy, drifting, and harsh.

Every millimeter of chassis vibration, every degree of thermal fluctuation, and every microsecond of network buffer bloat introduces distortion into the sensor stream. Most AI failures in the physical world are not model failures—they are ground-truth failures.

Mosaic Labs exists to guarantee that what the algorithm ingests is an exact, uncompromised representation of physical reality.
