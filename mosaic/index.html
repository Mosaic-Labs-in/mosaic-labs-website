The Continuous Data Cycle

The loop that connects machines to decisions.

Mosaic isn’t merely a dashboard or analytics layer. It is an intelligent system connecting physical systems to decisions—and feeding those actions back into reality.

STAGE 01 — REAL WORLD

Physical Systems Produce Signals

Vehicles roll, inverters switch, strain gauges flex, and environmental sensors sample reality. Physical assets continuously generate raw pulses of spatial, electrical, and thermal telemetry.

STAGE 02 — RAW DATA

Signals Become Fragmented Streams

Hardware clocks drift by milliseconds. Packets arrive out of sequence. High vibration causes intermittent packet drop and coordinate jitter across disparate protocols.

STAGE 03 — MOSAIC DATA LAYER

Streams Enter The Mosaic Layer

Mosaic's edge engine enforces hardware-level PTP atomic timing. Asynchronous feeds are reconciled into deterministic, lossless ring buffers with zero drift.

STAGE 04 — STRUCTURE

Data Organizes Itself

Spatial frames are calibrated in real time. Disparate sensor modalities are fused into a normalized geometric tensor lattice. Chaos becomes order.

STAGE 05 — INTELLIGENCE

Patterns Emerge from Structure

With ground-truth precision guaranteed, high-assurance machine learning models detect micro-anomalies, energy bottlenecks, and predictive failure signatures.

STAGE 06 — DECISION

Patterns Become Decisions

Autonomous policies synthesize immediate recommendations: dynamically re-route vehicle clusters, ramp down battery inverter heat, or tension structural cables.

STAGE 07 — ACTION & THE LOOP

Action Feeds Back to Reality

The decision actuates physical hardware in the real world. The physical system changes state, emitting fresh telemetry—and the intelligent loop begins anew.
