The End-to-End Pipeline

From fragmented signals to AI-ready datasets.

AI models are only as good as the data behind them. Mosaic sits between raw, fragmented physical-world signals and the AI systems that need clean, structured, trustworthy data to work.

STAGE 01 - RAW SIGNALS

The Physical World Generates Data It Cannot Use

Every industry runs on data trapped in paper documents, legacy PDFs, siloed equipment logs, or field reports. It is fragmented across vendors, unstructured, unannotated, and impossible for machine learning models to consume.

STAGE 02 - COLLECTION

Multi-Channel Ingestion: Download, Capture, Partner

We source data through whichever method is fastest and most defensible: downloading licensed public archives, capturing fresh telemetry via cameras, drones, and sensors, or partnering with utility and equipment operators.

STAGE 03 - PRE-PROCESSING

Validation, Deduplication & Early Redaction

Privacy and compliance are built into the pipeline at ingest. Personal data like faces and license plates is redacted at the point of collection, duplicate entries are pruned, and noise is filtered before storage.

STAGE 04 - ANNOTATION & STRUCTURING

Transforming Raw Signals into Schema-Driven Formats

Disparate imagery, spatial readings, and tabular records are normalized into clean, standardized schemas. Every asset is consistently tagged, segmented, and structured for downstream machine learning workloads.

STAGE 05 - QUALITY ASSURANCE

Auditing Against Rigorous Domain Benchmarks

Every dataset is checked against defined accuracy thresholds and integrity benchmarks before it ships. We verify label consistency, schema adherence, and boundary precision to ensure production reliability.

STAGE 06 - PROVENANCE

Full Chain-of-Custody Documentation

Every asset carries a transparent provenance report detailing its source, collection methodology, DPDP-aligned consent basis, and validation history so enterprise buyers can build with complete legal trust.

STAGE 07 - DELIVERY (DaaS)

Data as a Service, Continuous Feeds & Applied Models

Delivered as custom datasets, recurring subscription feeds, or applied AI models trained on customer data. Because datasets are continuously refreshed and versioned, their value compounds over time.
